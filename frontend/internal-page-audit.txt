DESIGN AND CONTENT CHANGES
- New styles are rooted at .internal-page only. The new class is on internal
  main elements, never on the homepage, header, footer or shared site shell.
- Navy introductions, warm-white headings, light reading surfaces and restrained
  blue/violet actions connect the internal pages with the homepage.
- Training retains learning topics and preparation guidance; consulting retains
  technical scope and integration considerations; recruitment uses a single-
  column scope list and a three-column overview on larger screens.
- All 13 offering pages have distinct overview headings and illustrative
  three-stage explanations derived from their existing catalog text. These are
  examples, not certification claims, guaranteed outcomes or fixed deliverables.
- The three category pages now explain how to choose the relevant offering.
- About keeps the existing company narrative and replaces only its decorative
  orbit with workplace photography. Contact keeps its actual submission flow;
  it adds required-field guidance and a warning not to submit confidential data.
- Removed pointer tilt from the internal service cards; existing preference-
  aware reveals, keyboard support and native FAQ disclosures remain.
- Responsive rules at 1040, 800 and 600px recompose heroes, scopes, workflows,
  forms and editorial columns. At narrow widths the form precedes supplemental
  contact guidance. Inputs are 16px, image dimensions are reserved, hero images
  load eagerly and the below-form contact image loads lazily.
